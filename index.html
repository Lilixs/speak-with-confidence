  3. Let’s fix this one thing first.我们先解决这一件事。There are many problems, but let’s fix this one thing first.
  4. I want to help, but I need you to lower your voice.我想帮忙，但请你小声一点。I want to help, but I need you to lower your voice so I can think.
  5. We see this differently, and that’s okay.我们的看法不同，这没关系。We see this differently, and that’s okay. We can still be good neighbours.
  6. Let’s take a break and try again later.我们先休息一下，晚点再试。We’re both tired. Let’s take a break and try again later.
  7. I’m not going to argue about this.我不打算为这件事争吵。I’m not going to argue about this. My answer is final, but I say it with respect.
